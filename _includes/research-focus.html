Research

Ultrafast machine olfaction

Developing compact electronic noses that exploit high-bandwidth readout, dynamic operation, and transient features to resolve odours on millisecond timescales.

Turbulent chemical sensing

Measuring how real-world plume dynamics are transformed by chemical sensors, using synchronized optical ground truth to characterize, reconstruct, and benchmark dynamic sensing.

Olfactory robotics & embodied AI

Turning fast chemical sensing into closed-loop perception: source localization, plume navigation, and learning and control under intermittent, history-dependent observations.
